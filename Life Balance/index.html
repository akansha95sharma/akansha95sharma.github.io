Life Balance

Workouts, habits & wealth · by Akansha Sharma

Life Balance helps you look after your body, your days and your money in one private app: home workouts shown by a 3D coach (including gentle Golden Strength sessions for seniors and Zero to Hero for beginners), daily habits, mood and sleep, weekly reviews, and a simple wealth tracker with monthly statements. Available in English and Hindi. Your information stays on your phone.
