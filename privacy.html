Privacy Policy for Dayzle

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Dayzle app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information when you use it on Android.

1. Information We Collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location.

2. Advertising

The App shows ads using Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. To show ads and measure their performance, AdMob may automatically collect certain information from your device, including:

  • Advertising ID (Android Advertising ID)
  • Device type, operating system version, and language settings
  • IP address and approximate location (country or region)
  • Information about how you interact with ads

This information is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Google may use it to show personalized or non-personalized ads, depending on your consent and device settings. Learn more: How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. Analytics

The App uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the App is used and to improve it. Firebase Analytics may collect information such as app usage, device type, operating system version, approximate location (country or region), and an app instance identifier. This data is processed by Google and does not identify you personally. Learn more: Firebase privacy.

4. Your Consent and Choices

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from within the App.

You can also reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your Android device settings under Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).

5. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each with its own privacy policy:

  • Google AdMob: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Analytics: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the App, please contact us and we will take steps to address it.

7. Data Security

We do not store your personal data on our own servers. Data collected by Google AdMob and Firebase is handled according to Google's security practices.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
