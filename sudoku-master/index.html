Sudoku Master

Sudoku & Arrows GO · by Akansha Sharma

Sudoku Master brings two puzzle games together in one app: classic Sudoku with seven difficulty levels, daily challenges, smart hints and statistics, and Arrows GO, a puzzle where you free arrows in the right order to clear the board.
