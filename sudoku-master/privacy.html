1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile app Sudoku Master (package com.sudokumaster.app), which includes the games Sudoku and Arrows GO, handles information. The app is published by:

2. The short version

  • You can play without creating an account. We do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number or location.
  • Your game progress and settings are stored only on your device.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier, subject to your consent choices.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We never see your payment details.

3. Information stored on your device

The following data is saved locally on your device so the app works and remembers your progress. It is not uploaded to our servers:

  • Puzzles in progress, notes and move history (for undo and resume)
  • Statistics such as games played, best times, streaks, achievements and daily-challenge history
  • Arrows GO progress such as current level, hearts and completed levels
  • Settings such as theme, sound, vibration and gameplay options
  • In-game coins and rewards, and a cached record of your purchases so premium features work offline

You can delete this data at any time by clearing the app's storage in your device settings or by uninstalling the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you have not purchased the ad-free option, the app shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited). To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple IDFA if you allow tracking)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Device and app information, and ad interactions (for example, views, clicks and completed rewarded videos)

Google's use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

5. Your consent choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before ads are shown, the app displays Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). If you do not consent to personalised ads, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Settings > Privacy Options.
  • iPhone and iPad: the app asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, your IDFA is not used.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Ad-free option: purchasing Sudoku Pro removes ads.

6. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (subscriptions and a one-time lifetime unlock) are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not receive or store your card or bank details. The store gives the app a purchase confirmation (such as a purchase token and order ID) so premium features can be unlocked and restored. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice or Apple's Privacy Policy for how the stores handle your payment data.

7. Analytics and crash reports

The app does not send analytics events or crash reports to servers operated by us. Google AdMob and the app stores may collect technical and diagnostic information as described in their own privacy policies. If we add an analytics or crash-reporting service in the future, we will update this policy before doing so.

8. Children

Sudoku Master is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

9. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. We do not keep copies of it. For data held by Google or Apple (for example, advertising or purchase data), please use their tools and settings, or contact us and we will help where we can.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store personal data on our own servers, most requests can be handled through the in-app privacy options and your device settings. You can also contact us at the e-mail address below.

11. International processing

Google and Apple may process data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under their own safeguards.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the services it uses change. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
