Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile app Life Balance (package com.vaibhavpandeycs.lifebalance) handles information. The app is published by:

2. The short version

  • You can use the app without creating an account. Your profile exists only on your phone.
  • Everything you enter (habits, mood, sleep, workouts, reflections and money entries) is stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive it.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising identifier, subject to your consent choices. Life Balance Plus subscribers see no ads.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Google Play. We never see your payment details.

3. Information stored on your device

The following data is saved in the app's private storage on your device so the app can show your progress. It is not uploaded to our servers:

  • Your display name, language, theme and other settings
  • Habits, routines and check-ins; mood, energy and sleep notes; reflections, intentions and weekly reviews
  • Money entries you type in: accounts, categories, transactions, plans and monthly statements
  • Workouts you complete, streaks and achievements
  • For outdoor walks and runs that you track: distance, pace and the route (location points)
  • A cached record of your Life Balance Plus purchase, so Plus features work offline

Your PIN is stored only as a protected value (a hash) in your device's secure storage. Fingerprint or face unlock is handled by your device's operating system; the app never receives your biometric data.

You can delete this data at any time in the app (Profile & Settings → Reset App Data), by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app.

4. Permissions

  • Location (including in the background): used only while you track an outdoor walk or run, to measure distance, pace and route. Background access lets tracking continue when the screen is off. Location is not used at any other time, is not used for ads by the app, and stays on your device.
  • Notifications: for reminders you set. They are created on your device.
  • Biometrics: to unlock the app with your fingerprint or face, if you turn it on.
  • Internet and advertising ID: used by Google AdMob to show ads and by Google Play to process purchases.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you do not have Life Balance Plus, the app can show two kinds of ads provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited): an optional rewarded video that you choose to watch to use a Plus feature for the rest of the day, and at most one full-screen ad per day after you finish a workout. The app shows no banner ads and no ads during a workout. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID)
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Device and app information, and ad interactions (for example, views, clicks and completed rewarded videos)

The app never shares your habits, mood, sleep, workout or money entries with Google or any advertiser. Google's use of advertising data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

6. Your consent choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: before ads are shown, the app displays Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform). If you do not consent to personalised ads, only non-personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the app under Profile & Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).
  • Ad-free option: Life Balance Plus removes all ads.

7. In-app purchases

Optional purchases (monthly and yearly Life Balance Plus subscriptions and a one-time lifetime unlock) are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your card or bank details. Google Play gives the app a purchase confirmation so Plus features can be unlocked and restored. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice for how Google handles payment data.

8. Analytics and crash reports

The app does not include analytics or crash-reporting services, and we do not operate analytics servers.

9. Sharing

You can create a progress picture and share it through your device's share menu. Sharing happens only when you choose it, and only to the app or person you pick. We do not receive a copy.

10. Health information

Life Balance is for general fitness and wellbeing. It does not give medical advice and is not a medical device. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program, especially if you are older, pregnant or have a health condition.

11. Children

Life Balance is intended for users aged 18 and older and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will help remove it.

12. Data retention and deletion

Data stored on your device stays there until you reset it in the app, clear the app's storage or uninstall the app. We do not keep copies of it. For data held by Google (for example, advertising or purchase data), please use Google's tools and settings, or contact us and we will help where we can.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, or US state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to or restrict its processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not store personal data on our own servers, most requests can be handled through the in-app options and your device settings. You can also contact us at the e-mail address below.

14. International processing

Google may process advertising and purchase data in countries other than your own, including the United States, under its own safeguards.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app or the services it uses change. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
