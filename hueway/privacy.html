Hueway Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

Hueway ("the app") is an offline puzzle game for Android and iOS. This policy explains what data the app handles. Contact: [your support email]

Data stored on your device

Your level progress, settings, lives and boosters are saved only on your device. There is no account, no login and no Hueway server. Uninstalling the app deletes this data; you can also reset it in Settings.

Gameplay statistics

If you opt in, the app records anonymous gameplay events (for example level started or completed, time and hints used) to tune difficulty. These events stay on your device and are not sent to us or to any third party. No name, email, contacts, location or device identifier is collected by Hueway itself.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner at the bottom of the screen, optional rewarded videos (watched by choice in exchange for lives or boosters) and occasional ads between levels. Ads are requested as non-personalised. To serve ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • App diagnostics and performance data (for example crash logs)
  • Ad interactions (for example ad views and taps)

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Data is encrypted in transit.

In the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads → Ad privacy options. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in the system settings. On iOS the app does not request App Tracking Transparency permission and does not track you.

Children

Hueway is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.
